I wanted a price tracker I could read in an afternoon and run from cron.

price-tracker is a small open-source Node CLI. List product pages in a JSON file; each run it fetches them through the Spicrawl API, compares price and stock with the last check, and alerts (console, Discord or Slack) on a drop, a target price or a restock.

It reads a page's JSON-LD when the shop publishes it, and a CSS selector you give it when not. One price per page.

A check costs 1 credit per product; the free plan has 1,000 a month, and failed requests cost 0.

Repo, live demo and demo video are in the first comment.
